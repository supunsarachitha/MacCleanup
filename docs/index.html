The workflow

You stay in the loop.

A scan only reads. Cleanup begins after you have seen the results and approved the selection.

  1. 01

    Scan

    See the size and item count for each supported location. Scanning changes nothing.

  2. 02

    Review

    Expand a category to inspect its location and a short preview of what was found.

  3. 03

    Select

    Choose categories yourself. Personal files in Downloads and Xcode Archives start unchecked.

  4. 04

    Confirm

    Check the totals in the confirmation dialog before any cleanup starts.

Built around restraint

Personal files are out of bounds.

The safety policy rejects protected folders, system locations, libraries, and anything that is not a direct child of an approved cleanup folder. Symlinks are not followed. Items changed after scanning are skipped.

Downloads and Xcode Archives that you choose to clean are moved to the Trash. Other selected cache and temporary items are deleted. Docker volumes are never included.

Permissions stay optional

Full Disk Access is only needed for protected locations such as Trash and Safari cache. The rest of the scan remains available without it.
